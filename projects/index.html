Featured Project 01

Sustainable Marin - Website Migration

I migrated Sustainable Marin from a custom build to WordPress so their team can update pages faster without relying on a developer for routine changes.

Challenge

Their previous site made basic edits slow because most updates required developer support.

Solution

I rebuilt the site in WordPress with reusable page patterns, clearer structure, and an editing workflow staff can use directly.

Outcome

I gave the team a self-serve CMS, faster publishing, and a lower-maintenance site with fewer routine dev requests.

  • I moved page updates from developer-needed to staff self-serve.
  • I standardized layouts in WordPress so edits stay consistent across pages.
  • I reduced long-term maintenance overhead for routine site updates.

Featured Project 02

VenturePad - Coworking Membership Site

VenturePad is an environmentally friendly coworking space headed by an environmental professor. It has hosted companies such as Pacific Sun, In Defense of Animals, and iNaturalist, serving as a launching point. I've worked with them over the course of 3 years to reduce complexity, improve image quality, and introduce new pages to their site.

Challenge

The site needed less complexity, better image quality, and new pages as the coworking space grew.

Solution

I redesigned the website layout from the ground up, then continued managing the sales funnel, newsletters, page updates, and SEO.

Outcome

Over 3 years of ongoing work: 17 pages updated and 278 new leads.

  • 3 years of continuous site management and updates.
  • 17 pages updated as the space and its members changed.
  • 278 new leads through the site.

Featured Project 03

MarqueeKit - Reusable HTML/CSS/JS Marquee System

I built MarqueeKit after struggling to find solid marquee examples for my own sites. It's a repeatable HTML/CSS/JS setup you can implement across projects.

Challenge

I couldn't find marquee examples that were smooth, easy to customize, and reliable across different sites.

Solution

I built a custom HTML/CSS/JS marquee pattern with repeatable structure, responsive behavior, and straightforward setup for new pages.

Outcome

Anyone can now go to marqueekit.com, purchase the solution, and implement it in their own projects instead of rebuilding marquee logic from scratch.

  • Built as a repeatable HTML/CSS/JS solution, not a one-off snippet.
  • Available as a purchasable product at marqueekit.com for direct project use.
  • Ongoing updates include performance tuning and maintenance fixes.
"Setup was clear, and the motion was smoother than the marquee's we used before."
